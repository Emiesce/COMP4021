QuickDraw Slayers

A real-time 2-player co-op card battle. There are no turns: both players act at the same time and every card takes effect the moment it is played.

How to play: click a card or press 1-2-3-4. Cards cost Energy, which refills by 1 every 2.5 seconds. Defeat every enemy before the 4:00 master timer runs out. Press C for cheat mode.
