1. The goal

You and a partner are Slayers fighting exam monsters. Beat the monster on each of the 3 floors before the 4:00 timer at the top of the screen runs out. Clear all 3 floors and you win!

It is real time: there are no turns. You and your partner both play cards whenever you like.

2. Play a card

You always hold 4 cards at the bottom of the screen. To use one, click it or press 1 2 3 4. It works instantly, and a new card is drawn to replace it.

Each card shows its Energy cost and its type: Attack hurts the enemy, Block protects you, Utility does something special.

3. Energy

Cards cost Energy, the blue pips at the bottom left. If you do not have enough, the card is greyed out.

Energy fills back up by itself, 1 every 1.8 seconds. The bar holds 4 on floor 1, 5 on floor 2 and 6 on floor 3. You start every floor with a full bar.

Tip: do not sit on a full bar. Energy that overflows is wasted, so keep playing cards.

4. The enemy attack timer

Under the enemy's name there is a red bar that counts down. When it reaches zero the enemy attacks you, and then the bar starts again. A full bar takes about 6 seconds (a little less on later floors).

So you have two choices before it runs out: defeat the enemy, or block the hit.

5. Defend yourself

You start with 100 HP. If it reaches 0, you are down.

Block cards give you Shield (up to 60). Shield takes the damage before your HP does. The best moment to block is just before the red timer runs out.

6. Team up

You and your partner both hit the same enemy, so help each other. For example: one of you plays Bell Curve so the enemy takes extra damage, and the other follows with a big attack.

See the Cards tab (the ? button) for every combo.

7. Between floors

After floor 1 and floor 2, a reward screen appears for 5 seconds. Pick one:

  • New card: a random card that costs 2 or more replaces the cheapest card in your hand.
  • Buff: +12% damage for the rest of the run, and +10 max HP.
  • Heal: recover 40 HP.

The main timer pauses while you choose, so take your time.

8. If you go down

Playing solo: if your HP reaches 0, the run is over (Defeat).

Playing with a partner: a downed Slayer watches while their partner keeps fighting. Clear the floor and they come back! If both Slayers fall, or the timer reaches 0, it is Defeat.

9. Cheat mode

Want to test the game? Press C to turn cheat mode on or off for the whole team. Both Slayers get unlimited Energy, take no damage, and deal triple damage.

A "CHEAT MODE ACTIVE" label shows at the top left, and your partner is told who changed it.

Attack cards

Attack cards hurt the enemy. Cheap ones are quick but weak, expensive ones hit hard. When your Energy is low, a cheap card now beats waiting for a big one.

Block cards

Block cards give you Shield, a layer of armor (max 60) that takes the enemy's hit before your HP does. Play one just before the red attack timer runs out.

Utility cards

Utility cards help in other ways: weaken the enemy, heal you, or give Energy. Group Project and Bell Curve also start a combo (see the next page).

The three bosses

One monster guards each floor. You meet all three every run, in a random order. They get stronger as you climb: floor 1 has HP, floor 2 and floor 3 , and every attack hits 7 harder on each new floor. In co-op, bosses have 80% more HP and attacks deal 25% more damage.

Tip: watch the red bar under the monster's name. When it empties, the attack lands, so block or finish it first.
