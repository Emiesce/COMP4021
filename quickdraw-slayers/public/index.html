How to Play

1. The goal

Two Slayers fight together in real time. There are no turns: both of you act at the same time and every card takes effect the moment it is played. Defeat the enemy on all 3 floors before the 4:00 master timer reaches 0.

2. Play a card

You hold 4 cards. Click a card, or press 1 2 3 4, to play it instantly. A new card is drawn to replace it.

3. Energy

Cards cost Energy. In solo, your Energy bar refills by 1 every 1.8 seconds. The bar holds 4 on floor 1, 5 on floor 2 and 6 on floor 3, so you can keep pressure on the enemy.

4. Enemy attack timer

Every enemy telegraphs its next attack with a countdown bar of fixed length. When the bar empties the attack fires at the Slayers, so block or kill the enemy before then.

5. Defend

Block cards fill your Shield meter. Shield soaks incoming damage before your HP does, so time your block for just before the enemy timer runs out.

6. Team combos

Work together: one Slayer applies a debuff and the partner follows with a big attack, or one shields while the other attacks.

7. Between floors

After floors 1 and 2 you get a 5-second overlay to choose a new card, a buff or a heal. The master timer is paused while you choose.

8. If you go down

In solo, reaching 0 HP is Defeat. In multiplayer, a downed Slayer spectates while their partner fights; clearing the floor revives them. If both Slayers fall, or time runs out, it is Defeat.

9. Cheat mode

Press C to switch cheat mode on or off for yourself: infinite Energy, no damage taken and 3x damage. A "CHEAT MODE ACTIVE" label shows at the top left and your partner is notified.
